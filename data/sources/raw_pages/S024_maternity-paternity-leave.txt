[MATERNITY] "If you are in paid work you will generally be entitled to maternity allowance,

F1. Maternity leave lasts 14 weeks and is paid. — evidence quote: "Maternity leave lasts

    allowance amounts to 80% of your earnings, but no more than CHF 220 per day."
