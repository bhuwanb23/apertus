=== SWISS FACT CONTENT ===
F1. Switzerland is a state that functions as federalist-organised direct democracy. — cross-ref: S025
F2. The National Council represents the people; the Council of States represents the cantons. — cross-ref: S002
F3. The National Council has 200 members and is re-elected by proportional representation every four years. — cross-ref: S002
F4. The Council of States has 46 members; each canton sends two, except the so-called half-cantons which send one. — cross-ref: S026
F5. Council of States members are elected by majority vote everywhere except the canton of Jura, which uses proportional representation. — cross-ref: S026
F6. The Federal Council has seven members, all with equal rights. — cross-ref: S001
F7. Switzerland has no president; the President of the Confederation is a primus inter pares who carries the title and chairs the Federal Council meeting. — cross-ref: S001
F8. The parties represented in the Federal Council (the 'Bundesratsparteien' / magic formula) are SVP, FDP and SP with two members each, and Die Mitte (formerly CVP) with one. — cross-ref: none
F9. Switzerland is a neutral state in foreign policy. — cross-ref: S038
F10. Switzerland has been a member of the United Nations since 2002. — cross-ref: S049
F11. Parliament accepted the UN Charter on 5 October 2001, and it became valid for Switzerland after the 10 September 2002 popular vote. — cross-ref: S049
