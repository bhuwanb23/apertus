=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. [CORPUS] "nöd" for "nicht", five times ("Es derigs, nöd zueverlässig eidüütigs Merchmaal"; "In aller Regel wird daas Kriterium nöd stringent aagwendet"; "Schpaatussiidler gelted dernaa nöd als Ussländer"). Zürich harvest: nöd 9, nid 0. Bernese harvest: nid 6, nöd 0. Basel [COMPARISON]: nid 14, nöd 0 ("het si Mäinig aber nid chönne duuresetze"). THE strongest lexical Basel+Bern vs Zürich opposition in the corpus. Use this as the headline Zürich/Bern contrast item.
C2. [CORPUS] "gits" = gibt/es gibt, twice ("I der Schwiz gits d Bezeichnig Bürgermeischter eso nümme"; "I jedere Gmeind gits als Vertretig eine, zwei oder drüü Vizebürgermeischter"). CONTRAST [CORPUS]: the Bernese harvest uses the unprefixed "git" four times and never "gits" ("Es git aber e Verein"; "Di gröschti Azahl vo Brauereie git's z Grossbritannie"); the Zürich harvest uses "gits" twice and never "git". Basel [COMPARISON] uses "git" five times. So this is a genuine g-loss / t-affrication opposition: Bern+Basel "git", Zürich "gits". A clean, usable three-way item.
C3. [CORPUS] "mer" = man ("Vor allem, wenn mer si in Europa betrachtet"). Bernese uses "Me"/"me" ("Me seit im ou Randblatt-Tabak"). Basel [COMPARISON] uses "me" ("is nüt me übrig"). Basel and Zürich overlap on the -r, so use only Bern vs Zürich.
C4. [COMPARISON] "Schtaatsaaghörigkeit" with the aa- diphthong vs Basel "Staatsbürgerschaft" with single a (e.g. als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fischmarkt_(Basel): "...zum Staatseigetum vo dr Helvetische Republik erchlärt"). Bernese has no form of this word in the harvest, so this is a Zürich-vs-Basel contrast only, not a three-way one.
C5. [CORPUS] Zeit word: "In jüngschter Zit" here, but the Zürich harvest ALSO has "Ziit" twice ("Nume i de Ziit vo dr Reformation"). Basel [COMPARISON] "Zit" 6 and Bernese [CORPUS] "Zyt". Conclusion: the Zeit word is NOT a reliable dialect discriminator - Zürich uses at least two forms. Explicitly recorded as a negative result.
C6. [UNVERIFIED] "Iiordnig", "Uusländer" double-u, "Aawendig" - no Basel or Bern cognate found in my sample. No cross-dialect claim made.
C7. [CORPUS] "chönnd" = können, same k -> ch spelling as Bernese "chöme"/"chöi". SHARED, not a marker.
