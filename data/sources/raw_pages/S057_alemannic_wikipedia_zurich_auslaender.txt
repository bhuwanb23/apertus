=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. "nöd" for "nicht" - occurs six times here ("nöd zueverlässig", "nöd stringent aagwendet", "nöd als Ussländer", "muess defür ... nöd zwingend"). VERIFIED CONTRAST: Bernese and Basel both use "nid" (Bern 30 / nid, 0 nöd; Basel 15 / nid, 0 nöd in my samples). Zürich "nöd" 40 vs "nid" 1. This is the strongest single lexical Basel+Bern vs Zürich opposition in the corpus.
C2. "Uusländer" with double-u. Not attested in the Basel sample under this spelling; recorded as Zürich-attested, no Basel claim.
C3. "Iiordnig" / "iiordnig" - the geminate ii- (in contrast to Bernese "Iiordnig"? my Bernese sample has none). Recorded as Zürich-attested only.
C4. "Zit" vs Bernese "Zyt". This article has "In jüngschter Zit" - ZIT, not zyt. Basel also uses "Zit" (6 occurrences). So "Zit" is shared between Basel and this Zürich article, and Bernese uses "Zyt" (2 occurrences, 0 "Zit"). VERIFIED three-way: Basel=Zit, Zürich=Zit (at least sometimes - the Zürich corpus has "Zyt" 6 and "Zit" 3, so Zürich is genuinely mixed), Bern=Zyt. Reported as a partial/variable difference, NOT a categorical one.
C5. "gits" - Bernese has "Es git aber e Verein" (unprefixed g-); Zürich "D Schwiiz nimmt ... " and "gits" appear in the Zürgermeister article ("I der Schwiz gits d Bezeichnig"). Shared. Recorded to prevent a false contrast.
C6. "mer" (man) - "Vor allem, wenn mer si in Europa betrachtet". Bernese "Me" ("bezeichnet me im wytere Sinn"). VERIFIED on both non-Basel sides; Basel not attested.
C7. "Schtaatsaaghörigkeit" - the aa- diphthong in the extended form. Basel uses "Staatsbürgerschaft"/"Schtaatsbürger" ("d Staatsbürgerschaft ... Staatseigetum" appears in my Basel Fischmarkt sample) with single a. VERIFIED: Basel has "Staats" (single a) and Zürich has "Schtaats" (aa). Bernese: my Bernese sample has neither form. Recorded as Zürich-vs-Basel, not Zürich-vs-Bern.
C8. "chönnd" (können) - shared with Bernese "chöme"/"chöi", so NOT a discriminator. Recorded to prevent a false contrast.
