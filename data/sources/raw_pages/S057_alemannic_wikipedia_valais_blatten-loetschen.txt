=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. "ischt" for "ist". Appears four times here ("gibüwwti cho ischt", "ischt zum eerschtu Mal", "Der Gmeipresidänt ... ischt"). VERIFIED CONTRAST: Basel, Bern and Zürich use "isch" exclusively in my samples (156/156/354, zero "ischt"); Wallisertiitsch has "ischt" 8 and "isch" 187 in its overall sample but uses ischt heavily in the highly-Valais-pronounced texts. Walser uses "ischt" 38. The devoicing is therefore an alpine (Walser + Wallis) feature and absent from Basel/Bern/Zürich.
C2. "Chilchu" = Kirche (long /uː/). CONTRAST WITHIN THE ALPINE GROUP, verified across two files: Walser (Issime) writes "Chilche"/"Chilhu", while my Wallisertiitsch sample elsewhere writes "Chirche" ("Evangelisch-reformiertu Chirche" in Kanton Wallis, "der christlichu Chirche" in Mittelalter). Here in Blatten it is "Chilchu", i.e. this article sits with the Walser-type form, not the type-typical Wallisertiitsch form. Genuine dialect variation inside the highest-Alemannic area - excellent test material.
C3. "Gmei" = Gemeinde (monophthong). Contrast: Bernese "Gmeind" and Zürich "Gmeind" (diphthong), Walser "Gmäind" (umlauted diphthong). THREE-WAY VERIFIED from the sample: Wallisertiitsch "Gmei", Walser "Gmäind", Bern/Zürich "Gmeind". This is the clearest three-way vowel contrast in the corpus.
C4. "Rottu" = Rhône. Local hydronym substitution; Basel sample also uses "Rottu" ("di Rottu") - shared, not a contrast.
C5. "mu" = man (indeinite pronoun), with final -u. Zürich uses "mer", Bernese "Me"/"me", Basel "me". VERIFIED across the sample set. This is a strong Wallisertiitsch marker.
C6. "z obrusch" = ganz oben, with the locative preposition "z" + adverb. Basel "z obe"/"zue"; not verified precisely - recorded as Wallisertiisch-attested.
C7. "heggschtalemannisch" - note the doubled g. The standard German term is "höchstalemannisch". This orthographic doubling appears throughout Wallisertiitsch text ("hegschdalemanisch" in the Walser Issime article). Worth flagging: Éischemtöitschu uses single g, Wallisertiitsch tends to double. Verified across both alpine files.
C8. PROVENANCE WARNING: this article documents the Blatten village destruction by rockfall in May 2025 and is therefore a recent, editorially-worked text. Register is more polished/standard Alemannic than the 2005-era Blatten prose would be. Dialectally still unambiguous (ischt, Gmei, mu), but treat style as "edited wiki prose", not spontaneous speech.
C9. IMPORTANT: the topic (alpine village geography) is the most distant possible from the Basel German material in the other agent's set. This is the highest-value item for maximum dialect contrast.
