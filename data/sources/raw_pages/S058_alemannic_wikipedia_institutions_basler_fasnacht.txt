=== SWISS FACT CONTENT ===
F1. The Basel Fasnacht is the largest carnival in Switzerland. — cross-ref: none
F2. The Basel Fasnacht lasts exactly 72 hours. — cross-ref: none
F3. The Basel Fasnacht is today regarded as the only large Protestant carnival in the world. — cross-ref: none
F4. The Fasnachts-Comité has organised the Basel Fasnacht since 1911. — cross-ref: none
F5. In Basel a 'Masgge' (mask) means a costumed figure — one that wears both a costume (Goschdyym) and a mask (Larve); the two words are not synonyms. — cross-ref: none
F6. The largest Fasnacht in the canton Basel-Landschaft is that of Liestal, drawing tens of thousands of spectators. — cross-ref: none
F7. Over 12,000 participants are organised into groups, plus about 6,000 unregistered 'wild' Fasnächtler. — cross-ref: none
F8. The earliest mention of the Fasnacht is from 1376, the 'Böse Fasnacht'. — cross-ref: none
F9. In 1529 the carnival period was fixed to the three days from Monday to Wednesday after Ash Wednesday — the reason Basel Carnival falls a week later than elsewhere. — cross-ref: none
F10. At the 1540 muster, 600 young men in armour with sword and halberd marched through the city. — cross-ref: none
F11. The Fasnachts-Comité issues a badge in four metal grades: copper (9), silver (18), gold (45) and the 'Bijou' (about 100). — cross-ref: none
