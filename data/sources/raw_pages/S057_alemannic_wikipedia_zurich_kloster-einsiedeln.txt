=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. [CORPUS] "nöd" for "nicht", twice ("Normalerwiis isch si für d Öffentlichkeit nöd zuegänglich"). Zürich harvest: nöd 9, nid 0. Bernese harvest: nid 6, nöd 0. Basel [COMPARISON]: nid 14, nöd 0. This is the single cleanest lexical Basel+Bern vs Zürich opposition available.
C2. [CORPUS] "mer" = man, three times ("mer hät s neui Chloschter i drü Etape bout"; "cha mer an e Chloschterfüerig"). Bernese uses "me"/"Me". Caveat: the Chindlistein file shows a single Zürich article using BOTH "me" and "mer", so treat me/mer as variable within Zürich, not a hard rule.
C3. [CORPUS] "Chloschter" = Kloster (6 occurrences). Bernese writes "Chlöschter" with the rounded vowel and plural -e ("In 9. Jahrhundert hei du d Chlöschter i der Schwyz afa Bier härstelle", in the Brauerei file). Bern "Chlöschter" vs Zürich "Chloschter" - both sides in-corpus.
C4. [CORPUS] "Chile" = Kirche (short form), 4 occurrences. Basel and Bern church-words are unattested in my harvest, so no three-way claim. Walser "Chilche" is a further form - see the Walser files.
C5. [CORPUS] "hüt" = heute ("is hüt di am beste erhalteni"). SHARED with Basel and Bern - explicitly recorded as NOT a marker.
C6. [CORPUS] Participle morphology: the doubled vowel of the participle prefix is visible here ("iigwölbt", "uufgnaah", "gmacht isch") and is equally present in the Bernese harvest ("ufgnoh worde", "befleidet händ"). SHARED Alemannic morphology, NOT a dialect discriminator. Recorded to prevent a false positive.
C7. PROVENANCE: the page's own dialect tag is "Züridütsch (See)". This article is NOT city Zürich - it is the Seedialect of the canton Schwyz. It is legitimately Züritüütsch by the wiki's own classification, but it must not be cited as evidence for city-Zürich features. Use the Bürgermeister file for those.
C8. [UNVERIFIED] "Münschter" = Münster has no attested Basel or Bern cognate in my sample. No claim made.
