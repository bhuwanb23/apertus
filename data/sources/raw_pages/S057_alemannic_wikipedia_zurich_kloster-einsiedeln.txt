=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. "nöd" for "nicht". VERIFIED CONTRAST: "Normalerwiis isch si für d Öffentlichkeit nöd zuegänglich". Zürich has "nöd" 40 times and "nid" once across my Zürich corpus; Bern has "nid" 30 and "nöd" 0; Basel has "nid" 15 and "nöd" 0. This is the cleanest single lexical Basel/Bern-vs-Zürich opposition I can evidence.
C2. "zuegänglich" vs Bernese "zuegspitzti" - both show the zue- prefix; shared morphology, not a contrast.
C3. "mer" = man (indefinite pronoun), attested twice ("mer hät s neui Chloschter i drü Etape bout", "cha mer an e Chloschterfüerig"). Bernese uses "me" ("bezeichnet me im wytere Sinn", "Me seit im ou Randblatt-Tabak"). VERIFIED on both non-Basel sides: Zürich "mer" is the standard everyday form, Bernese "me" the initial/singular form. Basel side not attested in my sample.
C4. "Chloschter" = Kloster. Bernese has "Chlöschter" ("In 9. Jahrhundert hei du d Chlöschter i der Schwyz afa Bier härstelle"). VERIFIED CONTRAST with the rounded vowel and plural -e: Zürich "Chloschter", Bern "Chlöschter". Basel side not attested.
C5. "Chile" = Kirche, short form. Bernese uses "Chilche" for the same word? My Bernese sample shows no church word. Walser uses "Chilche"/"Chilhu" and Wallisertiitsch "Chirche" - see the Walser/Valais files. Recorded here only as an attested Zürich form; the Zürich-vs-alpine comparison is evidenced in those files.
C6. "Büecher" (Bücher) with the ü-marked length vs my Bernese corpus, which shows "Büecher" only in Einsiedeln and no competing form; no cross-dialect claim made.
C7. "iigwölbt" (eingewölbt) and "uufgnaah" (aufgenommen) - doubled-vowel prefix on past participles. This prefix pattern also appears in the Bernese texts ("ufgna", "iigführt" is Zürich). Shared Alemannic morphology; NOT a discriminator. Recorded to prevent a false contrast.
C8. "Züridütsch (See)" - the page's own dialect tag. Important: this article is NOT city Zürich. It is the Seedialect of the canton Schwyz (Untervariante of Züritüütsch), at a monastery in Einsiedeln. It is legitimately Züritüütsch by the wiki's own classification, but it must not be used as evidence for "city Zürich German" feature claims. Use the Bürgermeister article for city Zürich.
C9. "Münschter" = Münster. Not attested in Bern/Basel samples; no contrast claimed.
