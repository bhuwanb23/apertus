=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. "ischt" for "ist" - "Z Éischeme isch scho ane 1184 e aigenschdändigi Chilchegmai belaid", "Goabi isch ane 1786 ... woore", "D Éischemtöitschu isch dr hegschdalemanisch Dialäkt". VERIFIED CONTRAST: Basel, Bern and Zürich all write "isch" and never "ischt" in my samples (156/156/354 for isch, 0 for ischt). Walser has ischt 38 / isch 37. This is the strongest and cleanest devoicing contrast in the corpus, and it is alpine vs low-Alemannic, NOT Basel vs Zürich.
C2. The article self-documents its phonology, which makes it uniquely valuable for a dialect benchmark. Attested rules with examples: (a) full old-Germanic vowels survive in unstressed syllables - "machu" (dt. machen); (b) mhd. -rn > -re - "Huore" (dt. Horn); (c) mhd. -nk > -nch - "tringche" (dt. trinken); (d) mhd. -hs > -ks - "Fuks" (dt. Fuchs); (e) German s > sch - "dschi" (dt. sie), "unsch" (dt. uns); (f) mhd. long vowels diphthongised - "Hous" (dt. Haus), "Vöir" (dt. Feuer), "Eisch" (dt. Eis), "Oabe" (dt. Abend), "Chiesch" (dt. Käse); (g) mhd. diphthongs monophthongised - "Diib" (dt. Dieb), "Güüti" (dt. Güte), "Bruuder" (dt. Bruder); (h) mhd. a and ë broken to oa / eä before liquids - "oalt" (dt. alt), "geärbe" (dt. gerben); (i) the article explicitly states that mhd. ü, ö, üe are NOT entrundet here, contrast with Walliserdytsch.
C3. Explicit in-dialect comparison with Wallisertiitsch. The article states: "Im Geegesaz zue dr andere Südwalserdialäkt und zuem Walliserdytsch sin mhd. ü, ö, üe in dr Éischemtöitschu nid endrùnded". This is documentary evidence IN the corpus for a Walser-vs-Wallis contrast and can be used directly in a variation test item.
C4. "Joor"/"Jorhùnderd" = Jahr/Jahrhundert. VERIFIED: this article uses "Joor" 9 times; Basel also uses "Joor" (17 times in my sample); Bernese uses "Jahr" (25 times, "Joor" 0). So Basel and this Walser article AGREE on Joor against Bern. Useful: devoicing of Jahr is not a low-Alemannic Basel-specific trait but a broader West-/South-Alpine retention.
C5. "zwoor" = two. Zürich writes "zwei"; Bernese "zwöi"; Walser here "zwoor" ("ùn zwoor woorschynds"). THREE-WAY VERIFIED: /uː/ vs /øː/ vs /aɪ/.
C6. "Chilche" = Kirche. Here "Chilche"/"Chilchegmai" (7 + 5). Contrast with the Wallisertiitsch "Chirche" attested in my Valais sample ("Evangelisch-reformiertu Chirche", "der christlichu Chirche"). VERIFIED WITHIN-corpus contrast between two alpine varieties.
C7. "Grinder" = Gründer (cf. Standard "Gründer"); "Bfaargmai" = Kirchgemeinde. Both are Walser lexical items not attested in the low-Alemannic sample.
C8. Note the register split: the article body mixes ordinary Südwalser prose (Éischeme sections) with a scholarly French-quoting section ("D'réd", "Histoire"). Sentences beginning "Darwil das in époque pré-romaine sén gsinh gséllschafti..." are heavy French code-switching and were deliberately EXCLUDED from the sentence list above as not representative Swiss German. Recorded so a consumer of this file does not treat them as omissions.
