=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. [CORPUS] "ischt" for "ist" is the dominant form in this article ("Z Éischeme isch scho ane 1184 e aigeschdändigi Chilchegmai belaid", "Goabi isch ane 1786 e aigeschdändigi Chilchegmai woore"). CONTRAST [CORPUS]: "ischt" occurs ONLY in the alpine dialects in the whole harvest (walser 2, valais 3) and is entirely absent from basel (156 "isch"), bern (24) and zurich (33). See Klosters C1 - this CORRECTS the brief's hypothesis that devoicing is a Basel-vs-Zürich split.
C2. [CORPUS] This article self-documents its own phonology with attested examples, which makes it the single most valuable Walser item for a dialect benchmark. Documented rules: (a) full old-Germanic vowels survive in unstressed syllables - "machu" (dt. machen); (b) mhd. -rn > -re - "Huore" (dt. Horn); (c) mhd. -nk > -nch - "tringche" (dt. trinken); (d) mhd. -hs > -ks - "Fuks" (dt. Fuchs); (e) German s > sch - "dschi" (dt. sie), "ünsch" (dt. uns); (f) mhd. long vowels diphthongised - "Hous" (dt. Haus), "Vöir" (dt. Feuer), "Eisch" (dt. Eis), "Oabe" (dt. Abend), "Chiesch" (dt. Käse); (g) mhd. diphthongs monophthongised - "Diib" (dt. Dieb), "Güüti" (dt. Güte), "Bruuder" (dt. Bruder); (h) mhd. a and ë broken to oa / ea before liquids - "oalt" (dt. alt), "geärbe" (dt. gerben).
C3. [CORPUS] The article contains an explicit in-dialect comparison with Wallisertiitsch: "Im Geegesaz zue dr andere Südwalserdialäkt und zuem Walliserdytsch sin mhd. ü, ö, üe in dr Éischemtöitschu nid endrùnded: Möisch (dt. Mäuse), Vüksch (dt. Füchse)." This is documentary evidence INSIDE the corpus for a Walser-vs-Wallis contrast and can be used directly as a variation item.
C4. [CORPUS] "Joor" = Jahr, 3 occurrences. AGREES with Basel (17), OPPOSES Bern (Jahr 4) and Zürich (Jahr/Jaarhundert). Same pattern as Klosters C4.
C5. [CORPUS] "Chilche" = Kirche, twice ("D Chilche ùn d Schuel sin bis in d 1920er Joor franzeesich gsii"; "Dydsch isch in dr Chilche ùn dr Schuel nie bruuchd woore"). The Wallisertiitsch articles in this harvest show NEITHER "Chilche" NOR "Chirche" (the Blatten file has the long form "Chilchu"), so the within-alpine Chilche/Chirche contrast is NOT demonstrable from the harvested subset. Wallisertiitsch "Chirche" does occur in als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanton_Wallis and /Mittelalter, which were fetched for comparison but NOT harvested. Marked unverified in-corpus.
C6. [COMPUS] "hed" = hatte, 7 occurrences. Zürich uses "häd" ("S Chloschter häd sit alte Ziit vil Ländereie"). A clean Walser/Zürich past-tense vowel opposition, both sides in-corpus.
C7. [COMPUS] "vù" / "vam" replace the low-Alemannic "vo": the Walser harvest has "vo" 0 and vù/vam 8. Basel "vo" 242, Bern "vo" 23, Zürich "vo" 37. Strong.
C8. REGISTER CAVEAT: the article mixes ordinary Südwalser prose with a scholarly section containing heavy French code-switching ("Darwil das in époque pré-romaine sén gsinh gséllschafti..."). Those French-mixed sentences were deliberately EXCLUDED from the sentence list as unrepresentative Swiss German. Recorded so their absence is not read as an omission.

C9. LENGTH NOTE: the sentence stating the ü/ö/üe non-rounding rule is 25 words and was therefore removed from the sentence list. It is the single most valuable in-corpus Walser-vs-Wallisertiitsch statement, so it is quoted verbatim in C3 above. Use C3 for that item; do not expect it in the sentence list.
