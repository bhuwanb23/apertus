=== VERBATIM SOURCE QUOTES ===
"There are three levels of political structure in Switzerland:

- communes (currently around 2,100)
- cantons (26)
- the Confederation (federal government)."

"Switzerland is a federal state. In practice this means that political power is shared by the Confederation, the cantons and the communes. Each level has its own special role to play in governing."

"Communes are the smallest political units in Switzerland. They exercise the powers delegated to them by the federal and cantonal governments, such as maintaining a register of the resident population and ensuring civil protection."

"The Federal Constitution is the highest law of Switzerland. It takes precedence over all laws and ordinances of the Confederation, cantons and communes."

"Cantons enjoy significant autonomy. The Federal Constitution ensures that all cantons are treated equally, and they all have the right to create their own cantonal constitutions."

"Any tasks that do not fall under the remit of the federal government are the responsibility of the cantons."
