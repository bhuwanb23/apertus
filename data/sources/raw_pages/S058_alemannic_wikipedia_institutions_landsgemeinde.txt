=== SWISS FACT CONTENT ===
F1. The Landsgemeinde is one of the oldest and simplest forms of democracy. — cross-ref: S004
F2. In a number of Swiss cantons the Landsgemeinde exists as a cantonal or regional institution, but in most cantons it has been abolished. — cross-ref: S040
F3. Citizens with voting rights assemble on a set day in the open air and vote publicly on the business put to them. — cross-ref: S005
F4. The Landsgemeinde can elect the cantonal government (as in Appenzell Innerrhoden), pass laws or approve the budget. — cross-ref: S008
F5. Voting at the Landsgemeinde is by show of hands, by small sword (Bajonett), or by showing the voting card. — cross-ref: S005
F6. The cantonal Landsgemeinde still exists only in Appenzell Innerrhoden and in the canton of Glarus. — cross-ref: S040
F7. Regional Landsgemeinde still occur in Appenzell Innerrhoden and in the canton of Schwyz; in Graubünden they have not occurred since 2016. — cross-ref: S040
F8. The Landsgemeinde was abolished in Zug and Schwyz in 1848, in Uri in 1928, in Nidwalden in 1996, in Appenzell Ausserrhoden the following year, and in Obwalden in 1998. — cross-ref: S037
F9. The modern Swiss Landsgemeinde resembles the Germanic Thing but derives from the medieval corporations, not from the ancient assembly. — cross-ref: none
