=== FACT CANDIDATES (for MCQ generation) ===
F1. Anyone living in Switzerland must hold compulsory health insurance with a Swiss
    health insurer. — evidence quote: "If you live in Switzerland, you must be insured
    with a Swiss health insurer."

F2. A person who has just moved to Switzerland has three months to arrange health
    insurance cover, and is already insured during that period; the same 3-month deadline
    applies to newborns. — evidence quote: "If you have just moved to Switzerland, you
    have three months in which to arrange health insurance cover. The same deadline
    applies to new-borns. You or your baby are already insured for this three-month period."

F3. Insured persons have free choice of insurer: they may register with any authorised
    company operating in the region where they live, because insurers are required to
    accept everyone for basic insurance. — evidence quote: "You can choose any company
    that operates in the region where you live. Simply register, because they are required
    to offer everyone basic insurance."

F4. Foreign retirees, students and diplomats are not automatically required to take out
    health insurance; exemptions are approved by the cantonal or municipal authority. —
    evidence quote: "Foreign retirees, students and diplomats do not necessarily need to
    take out health insurance. The relevant agency in your canton or municipality approves
    any exemptions."

F5. The ordinary cancellation deadline is 30 November, allowing an insurer change at the
    end of each calendar year. — evidence quote: "You can generally change your health
    insurer at the end of each year. To do this, you must cancel your basic insurance in
    writing. Your letter must reach your current health insurer by 30 November."

F6. A holder of the CHF 300 deductible under a standard/regular insurance model may
    additionally cancel with effect from the end of June, on three months' notice. —
    evidence quote: "If you have a deductible of CHF 300 under a 'regular' insurance model,
    you can also cancel your basic insurance with effect from the end of June. A
    three-month notice period applies."

F7. Basic (compulsory) and supplementary (voluntary) insurance can be held with different
    insurers, so switching basic cover does not force loss of supplementary cover. —
    evidence quote: "You can take out compulsory basic insurance and voluntary
    supplementary insurance from different health insurers."

F8. Health insurers are not obliged to accept applicants for supplementary insurance; the
    applicant must complete a health questionnaire. — evidence quote: "As a rule you will
    have to complete a questionnaire on your health before an insurer will approve
    supplementary health insurance for you."
