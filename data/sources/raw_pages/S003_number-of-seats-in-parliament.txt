TITLE: Number of seats in Parliament — How many seats does each canton have in Parliament?
CATEGORY: politics
LANGUAGE: en
DATE_ACCESSED: 2026-10-12

=== VERBATIM SOURCE QUOTES ===
"The more populated a canton is, the more seats it is entitled to in the National Council. In the Council of States, on the other hand, each canton has one or two seats."

"The Swiss Parliament has 200 seats in the National Council and 46 seats in the Council of States."

"In the National Council, seats are allocated in proportion to the resident population of a canton. This means that the more populated a canton is, the more seats it has in the National Council. Seats per canton range from 36 seats to only 1 seat. Each canton always has at least one seat in the National Council."

"In the Council of States, 20 cantons are entitled to two seats. The former half-cantons of Obwalden, Nidwalden, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel Stadt and Basel Landschaft are only entitled to one. This difference is due to historical reasons"

"Seat allocation in the National Council is based on population and reviewed every four years."

"In the National Council elections on 24 October 2027, the cantons of Lucerne and Fribourg will each have an additional seat to fill – bringing the total number of seats up to 10 and 8, respectively. The cantons of Bern and Graubünden will each have one seat fewer to fill (down to 23 and 4)."

"**Two seats:** Aargau, Bern, Freiburg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Luzern, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Vaud, Valais, Zug, Zurich."

"**One seat:** Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Nidwalden, Obwalden."

=== FACT CANDIDATES (for MCQ generation) ===

F2. Council of States: 20 cantons have two seats, the six former half-cantons have one each, for a total of 46. — evidence quote: "In the Council of States, 20 cantons are entitled to two seats... [total] 46 seats"

F3. F4. National Council seat allocation is reviewed every four years. — evidence quote: "Seat allocation in the National Council is based on population and reviewed every four years."

The cantons of Bern and Graubünden will each have one seat fewer... down to 23 and 4."
