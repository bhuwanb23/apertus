=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. [CORPUS] "vaner" / "vanu" = von, four times ("Im Taal vaner Lienne", "Dur ds Taal vaner Lienne", "98,2 % vanu Iwonru"). CONTRAST [CORPUS]: exclusive to the Wallisertiitsch harvest; basel, bern, zurich and walser have zero. Bern/Zürich/Basel use "vo" (23 / 37 / 242), Walser uses "vù"/"vam". THE most exclusive lexical marker in the corpus.
C2. [CORPUS] "gcheerunt"/"gcheert" = gehören/gehört, four times ("Zer Gmei Ayent gcheerunt di Deerfer..."; "Dr tradizionell Dialäkt va Ayent gcheert zum Frankoprovenzalischu"). Attested ONLY in the Wallisertiitsch harvest; absent from basel, bern, zurich and walser.
C3. [CORPUS] "Gmei" = Gemeinde. THREE-WAY CONTRAST: Wallis "Gmei" 3 / "Gmeind" 0; Zürich "Gmeind" 6 / "Gmei" 0; Walser "Gmäind" 1. Basel unusable (infobox noise).
C4. [CORPUS] Final devoicing ABSENT in Wallisertiitsch: "eerschtu" (2 occurrences, "zum eerschtu Mal") against Bernese "erscht" (2). The two forms differ only in the ending, so they form a near-ideal minimal pair for a devoicing test item. Contrast with Basel and Walser, which both write the devoiced "Joor" (Basel 17, Walser 3) while Bernese and Zürich write "Jahr"/"Jaarhundert". This is the cleanest devoicing test in the corpus - and it confirms the brief's Basel devoicing while showing Bern/Zürich and Wallis sit on OPPOSITE sides of the line.
C5. [CORPUS] "deerfer" = Dörfer. Not attested in the other four corpora. Recorded as Wallisertiitsch-attested.
C6. [COMPARISON] "Füesswäg" = Fussweg contains the -wäg element that also appears in Bernese ("Wäg sym Gruch", "der Wäg afa äbne"). So "wäg" is NOT exclusively Bernese - it is shared Bernese/Wallisertiitsch, and it is Zürich that lacks it. IMPORTANT CORRECTION to any assumption that "Wäg" marks Bernese specifically.
C7. [CORPUS] "mu" = man is in the Blatten file; the Ayent harvest itself has no pronoun of this class. Basel "me", Bern "me"/"Me", Zürich "mer".
