"Är isch der erschti Sohn vom Abraham u der Hagar u isch der Halbbrueder vom Isaak."

"Si chunnt ou der Uftrag über em Chind der Name Ismael z gä."
"Der Grund isch das Gott se i ihrem Eländ erhört het."
"So geit d Hagar zrügg u bringt der Ismael uf d Wält."
"Im Kapitel 17 chöme die zwöi e neue Name, also Abraham und Sara über."
"Bi dere Namesänderig, wird em Abraham verheisse das der Ismael zwölf Fürschte wird Züge."

"Si cha nid zue luege wi ihre Sohn stirbt."
"U dä isch e guete Jeger worde u het sech i der Wüeschti Pharan nider gla."

"Är het ds Gebiet vo Hawila bis Assur kontroliert wo ou d Handelsverbindig zwüsche Ägypte u Assur düren isch."

"Der Ismael stirbt im Alter vo 137i."

=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. "Frou(e)" = Frau. VERIFIED CONTRAST between the two non-Basel dialects: Bernese plural "Froue" ("zwüsche dene beidne Froue") and singular "Frou" ("vo syre erschte Frou"); Zürich writes the unaltered "Frau" ("synere Frau Mathilde"). Bernese drops the -w- in the plural. Basel side not attested in my Basel sample - no Basel claim.
C2. "geit" = geht. VERIFIED CONTRAST: Bernese "geit" appears twice here ("geit är uf dä Vorschlag y", "So geit d Hagar zrügg"), 7 times in my Bernese corpus, and 0 times in my Zürich corpus, where the verb appears as "gaat". Basel side not attested.
C3. "zwöi" = zwei. VERIFIED CONTRAST: "die zwöi e neue Name" here; 8 occurrences in the Bernese corpus, 0 in Zürich, which uses "zwei".
C4. "tuet" = tut. Bernese spells the short-u infinitive with -e ("Är tuet das nur"). Zürich shows "tuet" as well ("Ich tuet...": the Zürich corpus has "tuet" 5 and "tue" 3), so this is SHARED, not a contrast. Recorded to prevent a false contrast.
C5. "Jahr" not "Joor". VERIFIED: this article has "10 Jahr"; the Bernese corpus has "Jahr" 25 and "Joor" 0, while the Basel corpus has "Joor" 17. Basel keeps final devoicing, Bernese does not.
C6. "nid" for "nicht". VERIFIED: "dass me se nid cha zelle", "Si cha nid zue luege". Basel and Bern both use "nid" (Basel 15, Bern 30 in my samples); Zürich uses "nöd" (40 occurrences, "sind nöd vollschtändig deckigsgliich") with "nid" only 1. So the real contrast is Bern/Basel vs Zürich.
C7. "Wüschti Schur", "Felsi" type landscape vocabulary - Bernese/Biblical setting lexicon. Basel side unverified; no contrast claimed.
C8. "Är" for "er" (3sg masculine pronoun) - attested throughout this article. Zürich uses "er" ("Er wird je nach Land diräkt vo de Bürger ... gwählt", "Er vertritt d Gmeind au nach usse"). So Bernese "Är" vs Zürich "er" is a surface difference verified on both non-Basel sides. Basel side not attested in my sample.
