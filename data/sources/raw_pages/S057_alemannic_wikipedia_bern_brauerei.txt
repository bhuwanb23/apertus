S5. "Auch Wein wird fermentiert und trotzdem wird Wein nicht gebraut, sondern gekellert."
S6. "Für die Frauen war das Brauen so selbstverständlich wie das Backen von Brot."
S7. "Bern erteilte nämlich erstmals eine Bewilligung zur gewerbsmässigen Herstellung von Bier."
S8. "Das hat den Weg für die moderne Brauerei geebnet."

S12. "Ihre Geschichte reicht bis ins Jahr 1300 zurück."

=== DIALECT CONTRAST NOTES ===
C1. [COMPARISON] "Brot" in "Für d Froue isch ds Braue den so Sälbstverständlech gsi, wi ds Bache vo Brot." Basel writes the same word as "Broot" (als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basler_Brot: "S Basler Laibli isch e Broot us Heefidaig") and also uses the loaf-word "Laib" ("D Laib wärde noch em Ufgoo nume e chli gformt"). Final devoicing /d/ -> /t/ is absent in Bernese. Zürich has no bread word in the harvest, so no Zürich claim.
C2. [COMPUS] "Wäg" = Weg, twice ("Wäg sym Gruch wird är ou als Veielitabak bezeichnet"; "Das het du der Wäg afa äbne für di moderni Brauerei"). The Zürich harvest contains neither "Wäg" nor "Weg"; Zürich "Weg" occurs only in als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahngeschwindigkeit (fetched for comparison, not harvested). Recorded as Bernese-attested; the Zürich counterpart is not in-corpus.
C3. [CORPUS] "geit" = geht ("Ihri Gschicht geit bis i ds Jahr 1300 zrügg"). Bernese harvest: geit 3, gaat 0. Zürich harvest: geit 0 AND gaat 0 - the Zürich verb form "gaat" is attested only in als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enge ("Im Norde gaat si zum Täil na bis an Schanzegrabe"), fetched for comparison but NOT harvested. So the Bern-vs-Zürich opposition is real but only one-sided within this corpus. Wallisertiitsch also has "geit" (Ayent file) - so "geit" is shared Bern/Wallis against Zürich.
C4. [COMPARISON] "Zyt" = Zeit in "I der Schwyz stärbe wäret dere Zyt einigi Brauereie". Basel writes "Zit" (als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basler_Reformationsordnung: "Wie dr Gottesdienst in dere Zit usgsee het") and /Fischmarkt_(Basel) ("e langi Zit"). BUT the Zürich harvest also has "Zit" once ("In jüngschter Zit") alongside "Ziit" twice, so the Zeit word is genuinely variable in Zürich. Treat as a tendency, never as a categorical rule.
C5. [CORPUS] "Froue" = Frauen plural (6 occurrences across the Bernese harvest, e.g. "Für d Froue isch ds Braue..."). Zürich's "Frau" occurs only in als.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enge ("synere Frau Mathilde"), not harvested. Bernese drops the -w-; record as Bernese-attested.
C6. [CORPUS] "hüt" = heute ("Die Brauerei isch hüt e Tochter vo Carlsbärg"). Basel [COMPARISON] "Hüt isch si offe für alli Bruefsständ", Zürich [CORPUS] "is hüt di am beste erhalteni". SHARED across all three - explicitly recorded because "hüt" is frequently and wrongly listed as a dialect-specific marker.
C7. [COMPARISON] Basel "Bruefsständ" for Berufsstände shows the s -> sf shift absent in Bernese. Not attested in the Bernese harvest. Recorded for the Basel side only.
C8. [CORPUS] "Buur" = Bauer, singular, in the same article that elsewhere has "Buure-Tabak" in a different file. Both forms are therefore present in the Bernese harvest; Basel "Buur" is unattested. Recorded as register/variation within Bernese.
