NOTE ON THE PREMISE OF THE GAP:
This gap asked for TWO things: (a) the "Federal Act on Safeguarding Switzerland's Neutrality",
and (b) Congress of Vienna 1815 recognition. Only (b) could be verified from an accessible
official page, and it is recorded below.

(a) IS NOT RECORDED AS FACT. A dedicated federal-law neutrality statute was NOT verified on any
accessible official page. Notably, the accessible official sources point the OTHER WAY: the
Federal Chancellery and swissinfo state that Swiss neutrality is anchored in international
law and in the Federal Constitution, and no federal statute codifying neutrality was verified.
Treating "Federal Act on Safeguarding Switzerland's Neutrality" as an established fact would
contradict the sources below.
